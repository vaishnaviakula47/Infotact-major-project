# Time Resolution

The Autosync project contains climate observations recorded at different time periods.

## Time Information

The datasets contain date and time information associated with climate measurements.

## Time Resolution

The analysis should identify the available time resolution before comparing observations.

The observations can be organized according to:

- Date
- Hour
- Time period

## Importance

Understanding the time resolution helps ensure that climate measurements are compared at appropriate time intervals.

## Purpose

Time resolution information supports accurate temporal analysis of temperature and relative humidity data.
