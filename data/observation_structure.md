# Observation Structure

Each climate observation in the Autosync dataset represents a measurement associated with a particular time and sensor.

## Observation Information

An observation may contain:

- Date
- Time
- Sensor measurement
- Location
- Measurement type

## Temperature Observations

Temperature observations represent temperature conditions recorded by sensors.

## Humidity Observations

Humidity observations represent relative humidity conditions recorded by sensors.

## Purpose

Understanding the observation structure helps the project organize climate data for further analysis.
