# Temporal Alignment

The Autosync project uses climate observations recorded over time.

## Time Information

Temperature and relative humidity datasets contain date and time information that can be used to organize observations.

## Alignment Requirements

Before comparing climate measurements:

- Date information should be identified.
- Time information should be identified.
- Observations should be organized in chronological order.
- Measurements from corresponding time periods should be considered together.

## Purpose

Temporal alignment helps ensure that temperature and relative humidity measurements are compared for appropriate time periods.

## Importance

Correct time alignment improves the reliability of climate comparisons and microclimate analysis.
