How the Opportunity Score Works


AtmoSync currently uses a prototype analytical score based on the difference in temperature and relative humidity between two cities.

Opportunity Score = Temperature Difference + (Humidity Difference ÷ 10)

HIGH: Score ≥ 8    MEDIUM: Score ≥ 4    LOW: Score < 4

This is an analytical prototype for comparing micro-climate conditions. It is not financial advice and does not represent guaranteed trading or profit opportunities.
