Opportunity Scoring Engine
Current analytical prototype used to compare climate differences
AtmoSync compares every detected city against every other city.
The current prototype combines temperature and humidity differences into a single analytical score.
Opportunity Score = Temperature Difference + (Humidity Difference ÷ 10)

HIGH → Score ≥ 8    MEDIUM → Score ≥ 4    LOW → Score < 4

A higher score indicates a larger difference in the measured micro-climate conditions.

This is an analytical prototype for climate comparison. It does not represent guaranteed trading, financial returns, or guaranteed profit opportunities.
