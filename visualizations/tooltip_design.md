# Tooltip Design

Tooltips can provide additional information when users interact with dashboard charts.

## Tooltip Information

A tooltip may display:

- Location
- Sensor
- Date
- Time
- Temperature
- Relative humidity

## Design Guidelines

Tooltips should:

- Display relevant information clearly.
- Use readable labels.
- Avoid unnecessary information.
- Remain consistent across charts.

## Purpose

Tooltips help users inspect individual climate observations without overcrowding the chart.
