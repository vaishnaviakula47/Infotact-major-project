# Climate Comparison Chart Design

The Autosync project can use comparison charts to display differences in climate conditions.

## Temperature Comparison

A chart can compare temperature measurements between sensors or locations.

## Humidity Comparison

A chart can compare relative humidity measurements between sensors or locations.

## Time-Based Comparison

Line charts can show how climate measurements change over time.

## Comparison Information

Charts may display:

- Location
- Sensor
- Time
- Temperature
- Relative humidity

## Purpose

The chart design should make climate differences easy to compare and understand.
