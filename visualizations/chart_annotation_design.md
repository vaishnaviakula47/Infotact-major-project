# Chart Annotation Design

Annotations can be used to highlight important information in Autosync visualizations.

## Possible Annotations

Charts may highlight:

- Unusual temperature values
- Unusual humidity values
- Large climate differences
- Important time periods
- Potential opportunity results

## Design

Annotations should be placed close to the relevant data point or region.

## Purpose

Annotations help users quickly identify important patterns and unusual observations in climate data.
