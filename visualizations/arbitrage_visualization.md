# Arbitrage Visualization Requirements

The final project can use visualizations to present potential microclimate arbitrage opportunities.

## Required Information

The visualization may include:

- Location
- Time period
- Temperature difference
- Humidity difference
- Sensor comparison
- Identified climate variation

## Comparison

Locations or sensors with different climate conditions can be visually compared.

## Purpose

The visualization will help users understand where significant microclimate differences occur and how these differences relate to the project's arbitrage analysis.
