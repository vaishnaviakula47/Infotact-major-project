# Climate Difference Metrics

Climate differences can be quantified using temperature and relative humidity measurements.

## Temperature Difference

Temperature difference can be calculated by comparing measurements between sensors or locations.

## Humidity Difference

Humidity difference can be calculated by comparing relative humidity measurements between sensors or locations.

## Comparison

The differences can be examined across:

- Sensors
- Locations
- Time periods

## Purpose

Quantifying climate differences provides measurable information for identifying significant microclimate variations.
