# Climate Anomaly Detection

Climate anomaly detection can be used to identify observations that differ substantially from normal or expected conditions.

## Measurements

The analysis may examine:

- Temperature
- Relative humidity
- Sensor observations
- Time periods
- Locations

## Detection Process

1. Establish a reference or baseline.
2. Compare individual observations with the reference.
3. Identify unusual differences.
4. Record the location and time of the observation.
5. Pass identified anomalies for further analysis.

## Purpose

Anomaly detection can help identify unusual microclimate conditions that may require additional investigation.
