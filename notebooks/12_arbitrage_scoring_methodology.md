# Arbitrage Scoring Methodology

The Autosync project can use climate differences to create a structured opportunity score.

## Input Factors

The scoring process may consider:

- Temperature difference
- Relative humidity difference
- Location
- Time period
- Sensor variation

## Scoring Process

1. Calculate the required climate measurements.
2. Compare measurements between locations or sensors.
3. Identify the magnitude of climate differences.
4. Combine relevant indicators into an opportunity score.
5. Record the location and time associated with the result.

## Purpose

A structured scoring methodology can help organize climate differences into measurable analysis results.

## Output

The resulting score can be passed to the visualization and application layers for further presentation.
