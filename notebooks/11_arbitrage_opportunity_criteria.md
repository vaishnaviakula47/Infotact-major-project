# Arbitrage Opportunity Criteria

The Autosync project can use climate differences as inputs for identifying potential microclimate arbitrage opportunities.

## Possible Criteria

The analysis may consider:

- Temperature difference
- Humidity difference
- Location
- Time period
- Sensor variation

## Significant Differences

Large differences between locations or sensors can be flagged for further investigation.

## Analysis Process

1. Calculate climate measurements.
2. Compare measurements between locations or sensors.
3. Identify significant differences.
4. Record the corresponding location and time.
5. Pass the results to the visualization or application layer.

## Purpose

These criteria provide a structured basis for further arbitrage analysis.
