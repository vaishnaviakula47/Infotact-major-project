# Opportunity Thresholds

The Autosync analysis can use thresholds to identify climate differences that may require further investigation.

## Possible Threshold Inputs

Thresholds may be considered for:

- Temperature difference
- Relative humidity difference
- Combined climate difference
- Opportunity score

## Threshold Process

1. Calculate the required climate metrics.
2. Compare the metrics with defined thresholds.
3. Identify observations that meet the criteria.
4. Record the related location and time.
5. Pass the identified results to the next project layer.

## Purpose

Thresholds provide a structured method for filtering climate observations for further analysis.
