# Opportunity Validation

Potential climate opportunities identified during analysis should be reviewed before being passed to the final output.

## Validation Factors

The analysis can review:

- Location
- Date
- Time period
- Temperature difference
- Relative humidity difference
- Sensor information
- Opportunity criteria

## Validation Process

1. Identify a potential opportunity.
2. Check the associated climate measurements.
3. Verify the location and time information.
4. Review the calculated climate differences.
5. Record the validated result.

## Purpose

Validation helps ensure that identified opportunities are supported by the available climate data.
