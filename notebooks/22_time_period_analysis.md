# Time Period Analysis

Climate conditions can change across different time periods.

## Analysis Factors

The analysis may compare:

- Different hours
- Different dates
- Different observation periods
- Temperature measurements
- Relative humidity measurements

## Comparison

Climate measurements can be grouped and compared according to their recording time.

## Purpose

Time period analysis helps identify when notable microclimate differences occur.

## Application

The results can support further climate comparison and opportunity analysis.
