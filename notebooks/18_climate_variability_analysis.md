# Climate Variability Analysis

Climate variability describes how temperature and relative humidity measurements change across observations.

## Measurements

The analysis can examine:

- Temperature variation
- Relative humidity variation
- Sensor-level variation
- Location-level variation
- Time-based variation

## Analysis

Variability can be studied by comparing climate measurements across sensors, locations, and time periods.

## Purpose

Understanding climate variability helps identify locations or periods with different microclimate conditions.

## Application

The variability results can provide additional information for the climate comparison and opportunity analysis stages.
