# Temperature Analysis Methodology

## Mean
Mean temperature shows the average temperature recorded by a sensor.

## Minimum Temperature
Minimum temperature shows the lowest recorded temperature.

## Maximum Temperature
Maximum temperature shows the highest recorded temperature.

## Standard Deviation
Standard deviation shows how much temperature readings vary around the average.

## Sensor Comparison
Comparing sensors helps identify warmer, cooler, and more variable locations.

## Microclimate Analysis
These statistics help understand temperature differences between locations and identify microclimate variation.
