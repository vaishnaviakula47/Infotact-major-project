# Baseline Climate Comparison

A baseline comparison provides a reference for analyzing climate differences.

## Comparison Metrics

The baseline may consider:

- Average temperature
- Average relative humidity
- Temperature range
- Humidity range

## Comparison

Measurements can be compared between:

- Locations
- Sensors
- Time periods

## Purpose

The baseline provides a reference point for identifying meaningful climate variations.

## Analysis Flow

Climate measurements are summarized first and then compared with other locations or sensors.
